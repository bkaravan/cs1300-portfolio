Aspiring software engineer

I am a student at Brown who took open curriculum to heart. Among the trenches of Computer Networks, Operating and Distributed Systems, and Computer Graphics, I explored the design world with these case studies!

My goal is to become a well-rounded software engineer, so as a part of my Front End experience I made a deep dive into the world of Interaction Design!

Accessible components

A good software engineer understands components both as a user and as a developer. How different is a Google Drive search bar from the one in YouTube? Turns out, there are quite a few things to note!

  • Figma for State Modeling and Component Redesign
  • Front-end with HTML and CSS
  • Completed on 02/20/2025
Personas & Storyboarding

The next stop in our professional development journey is to take a more global look at design - when interacting with an interface, how will different users behave themselves? This case study takes a closer look at a self-ordering kiosk and creates a storyboard to model consumer behavior.

  • Community Figma file for Empathy Maps
  • Front-end with HTML and CSS
  • Completed on 03/06/2025
Responsive Redesign

I have always been amazed by the (potentially fictional) stories of people landing a technical job just to fix the problem nobody else got to. This case study gave me a chance to do just that: redesign a travel website that was frustrating to use.

  • Design panel and desktop, tablet, and phone mockups in Figma
  • Front-end in HTML & CSS, with special attention to accessibility and responsivness
  • Completed on 04/20/2025
Iterative Design

The last case study is a group project that focused on enhancing the design of certain features of an existing startup, an invaluable experience for aspiring software developers. The team went through a full design pipeline, from sketches on paper to an interactive Figma prototype.

  • Balsamiq wireframing
  • Figma for high-fidelity interactive prototype
  • Completed on 05/17/2025
