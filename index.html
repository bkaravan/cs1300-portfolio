I am a student at Brown who took open curriculum to heart. Among the trenches of Computer Networks, Operating and Distributed Systems, and Computer Graphics, I explored the design world with these case studies!

Let's take a deep dive into the world of interaction design!


Accessible components

Our journey starts at exploring different components that all of us are too familiar with. How different is a Google Drive search bar from the one in YouTube? Turns out, there are quite a few things to note!

  • Not all search bars are easy to navigate without a keyboard or a mouse
  • Using a voice-over device takes a lot of getting used to
  • It is challanging, but necessary, to think about accessable components!
Personas & Storyboarding

Our next stop takes a more global look at design - when interacting with an interface, how will different users behave themselves? This case study takes a closer look at a self-ordering kiosk and creates a storyboard to model consumer behavior.

  • My first time doing field research!
  • People like choice, but at what point does it become overwhelming?
  • Empathy Maps & Storyboards provide deep insights about consumers
Responsive Redesign

I have always been amazed by the (potentially fictional) stories of people landing a technical job just to fix the problem nobody else got to. This case study gave me a chance to do just that: redesign a travel website that was frustrating to use.

  • An unnoticed feature error can create a lot of negative user experience
  • Even the smallest interactions require thought-through design
  • Good user experience comes from both appropriate design and competent technical ability
Iterative Design

The last case study is a group project that focused on enhancing the design of certain features of Allara Health. The team went through a full design pipeline, from sketches on paper to an interactive Figma prototype.

  • Working with a start-up is a valuable life experience
  • Multiple design stages allowed for insights about user expectations and pain points
  • It is crucial to be flexible to change and have forward-looking mindset
